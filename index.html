01 Our philosophy

Layers of craft, flavour and everyday ritual.

Layers is a bakery and deli focused on freshly baked goods, specialty coffee, laminated pastries, slow-fermented breads, cakes and carefully prepared food. We believe a good morning starts with a good croissant — and a good croissant starts the night before.

L / B / D EST. 24
Hands at work — 06:14, Amsterdam.
02 The craft

Made slowly.
Served simply.

Every loaf, every pastry, every plate starts with time. Doughs ferment for thirty-six hours. Coffee is roasted a few streets away. Produce arrives from growers we know by name. Nothing here is hurried — and you can taste it.

We work in layers. A laminated croissant has twenty-seven. A sandwich is built one decision at a time. It's how we think about every part of the day, from your first flat white to a long, easy lunch.

06 About Layers

More than a bakery.

Layers brings together the craft of a bakery, the ease of a neighbourhood café and the energy of a deli. A place to start the morning, take a break, and stay a while.

  • 01

    Baked daily

    Pastries out of the oven from 07:30. Bread all morning. Cakes all day.

  • 02

    Good coffee

    Roasted locally, brewed with care. Filter, espresso, or batch.

  • 03

    Local energy

    A small, considered space in the heart of Amsterdam — built for the neighbourhood.

  • 04

    Slow food

    Made in-house, from scratch, with growers and roasters we know by name.
